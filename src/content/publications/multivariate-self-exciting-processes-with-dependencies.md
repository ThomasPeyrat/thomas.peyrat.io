We introduce the class of multivariate self-exciting processes with dependencies (MSPD), a unifying formulation that covers counting, loss and intensity processes, as well as shifted processes. The framework accounts for dynamic dependencies between the frequency and the severity components of the risk: the intensity $\lambda^i_t$ of component $i$ may depend on the marks of all past events. These dependencies raise theoretical challenges in the computation of risk valuations, which we address.
