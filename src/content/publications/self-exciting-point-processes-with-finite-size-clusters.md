We introduce a new parametric family of self-exciting point processes whose cluster representation has almost surely finite size. The construction yields closed-form expressions for key quantities while preserving the self-exciting structure, and offers a flexible alternative to standard Hawkes processes for applications in insurance and risk modelling.
